Welcome To e-Gazette


Director, Govt. Printing and Stationery is a nodal agency for printing and publishing Gazettes of Govt. of Gujarat under Industries and Mines Department.

The Gazettes are published from Gandhinagar Govt. Central Press, Vadodara Govt. Press and Rajkot Govt. Press.

Ordinary and Extra-Ordinary Gazettes of various departments and offices are published from the above three Govt. Presses.

As part of Digital Governance initiative of Govt. of Gujarat and to make various Gazettes easily available for public and also for Govt. Offices, free of cost, Industries and Mines Department vide GR Dated 03/07/2021 has discontinued physical printing of Gazettes and started online publication of e-Gazette.
